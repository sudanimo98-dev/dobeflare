Identity & Account Signals
✓
New Account (<7 days old)
Account created within the last week
why
+15
New accounts haven't established behavioral history. Combined with other signals, recency is a strong amplifier — most synthetic accounts are used immediately after creation before fraud systems build a profile on them.
✓
KYC Mismatch
Name, email, or DOB variation across linked accounts
why
+12
Fraudsters often use slight variations — typo emails (john@gmail vs john1@gmail), transposed names — to avoid exact-match deduplication. Fuzzy KYC matching across accounts sharing a device or IP is a strong cluster signal.
Device & Network Signals
✓
Headless Browser / API Fingerprint
Automated client detected — no real browser session
why
+22
Headless browsers (Puppeteer, Selenium) and direct API calls lack standard browser attributes — no canvas fingerprint, missing navigator fields, no human interaction timing. This is a near-definitive bot signal when combined with account creation activity.
✓
Shared Device ID
Same device fingerprint across 3+ accounts
why
+20
Legitimate users occasionally share devices, but 3+ distinct accounts on one device is outside normal behavior. Device ID sharing is harder to spoof than IP, making it a higher-confidence signal for synthetic account farms.
✓
Shared IP Across Accounts
Same IP used by 3+ different account IDs
why
+15
Shared IPs alone are weak (NAT, VPNs, shared wifi) but become significant when combined with other signals. Residential proxy IPs rotating across accounts at high velocity are a stronger sub-signal than static datacenter IPs.
Payment Signals
✓
Shared Payment Instrument
Same card / account used across multiple account IDs
why
+25
One payment instrument across multiple accounts is the clearest sign of coordinated fraud. In legitimate use, card sharing happens within families but rarely across unrelated accounts. This is the primary signal for carding rings and payment fraud clusters.
✓
Velocity Spike
3+ transactions from same account in under 10 minutes
why
+18
Fraudsters move fast — they know stolen credentials or cards have a short window before fraud detection kicks in. Rapid-fire transactions in a compressed window (especially across different merchants or amounts) is a strong behavioral signal distinct from normal purchase velocity.
✓
Chargeback History on Linked Accounts
Prior chargebacks on accounts sharing device / IP / card
why
+18
Historical chargebacks on a cluster of accounts are a lagging but high-confidence signal. If linked accounts have already generated chargebacks, new accounts in the same cluster are almost certainly part of the same operation. This anchors the current activity to a known bad pattern.
