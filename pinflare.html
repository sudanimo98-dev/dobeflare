Sampling Method
Random Sampling
Select items uniformly at random from the review queue. No bias toward any content type.
Stratified Sampling
Sample proportionally from each content category. Ensures every violation type is represented.
Risk-Weighted Sampling
Oversample high-risk and low-prevalence content. Prioritizes queues where misses cause the most harm.
Adversarial Sampling
Deliberately include policy boundary cases and known reviewer blind spots. Stress-tests calibration.
